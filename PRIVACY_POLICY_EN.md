# Privacy Policy for 小学コレ！理科 (Shokollen Science)

**Effective Date:** June 17, 2026  
**Last Updated:** June 17, 2026

## Overview

小学コレ！理科 ("App") is a mobile application designed to help elementary school students (grades 3-6) learn science. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Account Information
- **Anonymous Authentication**: We use Firebase Anonymous Authentication. No personal email or account credentials are stored.

### 2. Learning Progress Data
- Quiz answers and scores
- Completed stages and learning modules
- Daily login streaks
- Badge achievements
- Character collection status
- In-app currency and purchases

This data is stored both locally on your device (via SharedPreferences) and on our Firebase Firestore database.

### 3. Device Information
- Device type, OS version, and app version
- General location information (region/country based on app language settings)
- Analytics data (app usage patterns, feature interactions)

Collected via Firebase Analytics for app improvement purposes only.

### 4. Payment Information
- In-app purchase transactions
- Subscription status (if applicable)

Processed by RevenueCat and Apple/Google Play billing systems. We do not directly store payment card details.

## How We Use Your Information

- **App Functionality**: To save progress, track learning achievements, and enable offline usage
- **Analytics**: To understand user behavior and improve app features
- **Bug Fixes**: To diagnose technical issues and improve stability
- **Personalization**: To customize difficulty levels and learning recommendations

## Data Security

- All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption
- Firebase Firestore is protected by security rules (user-specific access only)
- Data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure with industry-standard security

## Your Rights
